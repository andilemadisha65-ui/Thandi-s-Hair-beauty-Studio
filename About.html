About Thandi

A story of passion, artistry, and beauty.

Our Story

Thandi's Beauty & Studio was born from a lifelong love for aesthetics and self-care. What started as a small home-based passion project has blossomed into a full-service beauty studio trusted by hundreds of clients.

Thandi believes that beauty is not about changing who you are — it is about enhancing what you already have. Every treatment is tailored to the individual, ensuring you leave feeling confident, radiant, and truly yourself.

Why Choose Us?

With years of hands-on experience and continuous training in the latest beauty trends, Thandi combines skill with a personal touch. The studio is a judgment-free zone where every client is treated like family.
